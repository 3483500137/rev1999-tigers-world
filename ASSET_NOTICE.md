Reference archive and scene-data arrangement: VioletWilde/1999-demo.
Source: https://github.com/VioletWilde/1999-demo
Pinned commit: 1d83ddc454c9c7c193e4c458412db42bfc5d8031

Included source assets: 11 WebP files under public/assets/all, chapter 104 scene data,
and game-derived material embedded in public/models/tigers-world.glb and
blender/tigers-world.blend. The Blender file also contains the original sandtable.

These materials are excluded from the MIT license. Public availability is not
a grant of commercial rights. This repository does not claim ownership of the
original game art or grant a sublicense to it.

The explorable geometry is reconstructed from the visible reference, not
extracted original game geometry. Visible architectural features are reference-guided;
unseen surfaces, rear passages and terrain heights are adapted designs explicitly
requested for free exploration. Pixel-perfect or face-for-face fidelity is not claimed.
No image-generation service was used; the original atlas patch is reused through UVs.

Each imported original texture has a recorded checksum in public/data/sources.json.
